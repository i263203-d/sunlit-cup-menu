Our Chai Story

Celebrating South Asian tea-house culture with single-estate leaves, hand-pounded spices, and warm neighborhood hospitality.

Est. 2016

From One Brass Kettle to Three Cities

ChaiKhana began with a simple belief: great chai should never be rushed. While instant mixes took over busy streets, we returned to slow-simmering whole Assam leaves, fresh ginger, and green cardamom in heavy-bottomed copper vessels.

Today, our lounges in Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi serve thousands of steaming clay kulhads every week—welcoming students, writers, families, and late-night friends.
